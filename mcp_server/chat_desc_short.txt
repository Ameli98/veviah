Make the user's 3D avatar say a line and move. Call once per reply. Tool results are internal notes: never mention them to the user.

Arguments: say (the line), emotion (neutral | happy | angry | sad | shy | surprised), posture (leave it out to keep the current one; "stand" to stand up; sitting: cross_legged, seiza, w_sit, side_right, side_left, knees_hugged, legs_out, kneel), keyframes.

keyframes = a list of 3 to 6 objects. Each has "t" (seconds, starting at 0, increasing) and only the parts that change:
- "head": {"nod": deg (+ down), "turn": deg (+ to her left), "tilt": deg}
- "torso": {"lean": deg (+ forward), "turn": deg, "bend": deg}
- "right_arm" / "left_arm": EITHER the string "rest" (arm hanging, the normal pose)
    OR {"hand": "HEIGHT ACROSS REACH", "palm": P, "wrist": deg (optional bend)}  with exactly three words:
       HEIGHT = above_head | head | face | shoulder | chest | waist | hip | low
       ACROSS = other_side | center | front | out | wide
       REACH  = back | beside | close | mid | far
       P = forward | back | up | down | in | out | natural
    OR {"touch": REGION, "with": palm | fingertips | fingers | fist | index}
       REGION = top_of_head, forehead, cheek, chin, mouth, chest, heart, belly, hip, waist, thigh, knee, back_of_head, other_shoulder, ...
- "hands_meet": {"at": "HEIGHT REACH", "how": "palms"}   (both hands together, two words only; do not also give right_arm / left_arm)
- "hands": {"right": relaxed | open | fist | point | peace | thumbs_up}
- "eyes": "closed" or "open"
- "legs" (standing only): {"weight": "left" | "right", "turn": deg (180 = her back to the viewer), "jump": "small"}

Always start and end with both arms "rest". Repeated actions (waving, nodding) need one keyframe per swing.
